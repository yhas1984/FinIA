## Full description

```
FinAI turns photos and everyday phrases into organized financial records. Track expenses and income, scan receipts, invoices, and payslips, and check your balance in an app built to reduce repetitive entry while keeping every detail visible.

AI features require your own Gemini API key and an internet connection. You can also enter expenses and income manually.

CAPTURE DOCUMENTS WITH AI
Take a photo, choose an image or PDF, or share a document to FinAI from another app. Send one invoice or payslip per PDF, with up to 20 pages and 10 MiB. FinAI can extract the merchant or issuer, date, invoice number, total, subtotal, tax IDs, taxes, and product lines. It also recognizes income documents such as payslips and sales invoices.

Reading and registration happen in chat. When the document amount can be read, FinAI saves the record automatically without a mandatory review screen. You can edit the result. An unreadable document can be retried; a repeated document prompts a warning.

AVOID REPEATED RECORDS
FinAI checks document identity before saving. When it detects an invoice or payslip that may already be registered, it warns you and lets you open the existing record.

KEEP THE FULL TAX BREAKDOWN
One invoice can contain several tax rates, exempt amounts, zero-rated items, or withholdings. FinAI keeps the components printed on the document instead of reducing them to one percentage. You can review the extracted breakdown and edit the document’s tax components when needed.

ADD RECORDS AND ASK NATURALLY
Type or dictate phrases such as “I spent $20 on transport” or “I received my salary.” You can also ask how much you spent, which income you received, or what your balance is. Calculations use your saved records and indicate when a currency conversion is unavailable.

YOUR FINANCIAL ACTIVITY IN ONE PLACE
• Monthly dashboard with expenses, income, balance, and recent activity.
• Create, rename and archive categories and their subcategories.
• Merchant rules for future transactions and monthly category limits.
• Edit expense and income records; view their product details.
• Calendar and statistics by period and category.
• Support for multiple currencies and number formats.
• CSV and PDF reports filtered by date, type, category and subcategory.
• Correct individual transactions in chat and undo eligible corrections.

GOOGLE DRIVE AND GOOGLE SHEETS
With Premium, sync from FinAI to one Google Sheets workbook: expenses, income, products, taxes and summary, with month and year filters and space for your own analysis. Personal sheets and columns are preserved. Automatically store expense and income original images and PDFs in Drive. Encrypted automatic backups contain data and references; photos sync separately.

OPTIONAL WALLET CAPTURE
With explicit Android notification access, record supported future Google Wallet payment notices on this device. Notices require a clear amount, currency and merchant. Original notification text is not saved or sent to AI. Receipt matching requires confirmation; taxes are not inferred from a payment. You can turn this off at any time.

ENCRYPTED, RECOVERABLE BACKUPS
Export a password-protected .finai file. Choose a data-only backup or a complete copy with original documents. Backups preserve records, products, history, tax settings, and the identifiers needed to retrieve linked images.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
Your records and history are stored on your device. AI features send the text, document and context needed for your request to Gemini, using the API key you configure. Drive and Sheets are used only when you connect your Google account and authorize those features.

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 8.0 or later.
• Your own Gemini API key for chat, voice, and document reading.
• Internet for AI, exchange rates, and Google services.
• Some Drive, Sheets, and extended memory features require Premium.

FinAI helps you enter less by hand and keep expenses, income, and documents under control.
```
