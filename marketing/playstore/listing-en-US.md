FinAI: Expense Tracker — your personal finance app with AI. Forget spreadsheets: track your daily expenses, scan invoices, and understand your money by talking to the app like a financial advisor. Built for freelancers and small businesses that need to manage daily expenses and income and keep their budget under control.

📷 SCAN INVOICES WITH YOUR CAMERA
Take a photo of any invoice or receipt to scan its data instantly. The AI automatically extracts vendor, date, invoice number, total, tax base, VAT, tax ID, and products with prices. Supports expenses and income: paychecks, sales invoices, and receipts. No typing needed.

🤖 AI ASSISTANT
Powered by Gemini 3.7 Flash to track your expenses naturally. Say "I spent $20 on coffee" or "I got paid my salary" and it's recorded instantly. Ask "How much did I spend this month?" or "What's my balance?" and get the answer right away. The AI remembers the conversation context and suggests categories and subcategories automatically.

🎙️ VOICE COMMANDS
Dictate your transactions while driving or cooking: speech becomes text and the AI processes it instantly.

📊 FINANCIAL DASHBOARD
Monthly balance, income, expenses, and weekly activity at a glance. Interactive statistics with percentages, a monthly financial calendar, and reorderable widgets to see only what matters. Understand where your money goes, adjust your budget… and save more.

📋 COMPLETE EXPENSE & INCOME MANAGEMENT
Invoices with vendor, invoice number, date, VAT, tax ID, and photo. Income with concept, gross, net, currency, and source. Products with quantity, unit price, and subtotal. Custom categories and subcategories, with country-specific tax configuration: Spain, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile, Peru, and more.

☁️ GOOGLE SHEETS EXPORT
Export your data to a Google Sheet with 4 tabs: Expenses, Income, Products, and Summary with automatic formulas. Once linked, every new expense or income syncs automatically.

💾 GOOGLE DRIVE BACKUP
Encrypted (AES-256) backup of your database to your personal Drive, with previous versions and restore. Export to CSV or PDF to share via email or WhatsApp.

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
Your financial data is stored exclusively on your device. No ads, no trackers. Google Sheets and Drive require your explicit OAuth authorization and only access files created by the app. No registration required.

REQUIREMENTS
Android 8.0+. Internet required for the AI assistant (messages are sent to the Gemini API). Free Gemini API key configurable in Settings. Camera and microphone optional.

Download FinAI for free and take control of your money: track, scan invoices, and save every month.
