## Full description

```
FinAI turns photos and everyday phrases into organized financial records. Track expenses and income, scan receipts, invoices, and payslips, and check your balance in an app built to reduce repetitive entry while keeping every detail visible.

CAPTURE DOCUMENTS WITH AI
Take a photo or choose an image. FinAI can extract the merchant or issuer, date, invoice number, total, subtotal, tax IDs, taxes, and product lines. It also recognizes income documents such as payslips and sales invoices.

Every reading is checked before it affects your finances. Coherent records are saved automatically. If information is missing or the amounts do not add up, the photo stays pending so you can retry without adding uncertain figures to your balance.

AVOID REPEATED RECORDS
FinAI checks document identity before saving. When it detects an invoice or payslip that may already be registered, it warns you and lets you open the existing record.

KEEP THE FULL TAX BREAKDOWN
One invoice can contain several tax rates, exempt amounts, zero-rated items, or withholdings. FinAI keeps the components printed on the document instead of reducing them to one percentage. You can review and edit each value whenever needed.

ADD RECORDS AND ASK NATURALLY
Type or dictate phrases such as “I spent $20 on transport” or “I received my salary.” You can also ask how much you spent, which income you received, or what your balance is. Calculations use your saved records and indicate when a currency conversion is unavailable.

YOUR FINANCIAL ACTIVITY IN ONE PLACE
• Monthly dashboard with expenses, income, balance, and recent activity.
• Custom categories and subcategories.
• Editable expense, income, and product records.
• Calendar and statistics by period and category.
• Support for multiple currencies and number formats.
• CSV and PDF exports.

GOOGLE DRIVE AND GOOGLE SHEETS
With Premium, sync from FinAI to one Google Sheets workbook: expenses, income, products, taxes and summary, with month and year filters and space for your own analysis. Personal sheets and columns are preserved. Automatically store expense and income photos in Drive. Encrypted automatic backups contain data and references; photos sync separately.

ENCRYPTED, RECOVERABLE BACKUPS
Export a password-protected .finai file. Choose a data-only backup or a complete copy with photos. Backups preserve records, products, history, tax settings, and the identifiers needed to retrieve linked images.

PRIVACY AND CONTROL
Your records and history are stored on your device. AI features send the text, document and context needed for your request to Gemini, using the API key you configure. Drive and Sheets are used only when you connect your Google account and authorize those features.

REQUIREMENTS
• Android 8.0 or later.
• Your own Gemini API key for chat, voice, and document reading.
• Internet for AI, exchange rates, and Google services.
• Some Drive, Sheets, and extended memory features require Premium.

FinAI helps you enter less by hand and keep expenses, income, and documents under control.
```
